About Jake

I was working hard. I just wasn't getting the results I wanted.

As a college athlete, I spent a lot of time training and learning about fitness. But what I really wanted was simple: to build a physique I was proud of. The harder I tried and the more information I consumed, the more frustrated I became. I was putting in the effort without a clear understanding of what actually mattered.

That pushed me to study Human Physiology and Preventive Medicine, work with my own coaches, and learn from mentors who challenged how I thought about training and nutrition. I learned that progress doesn't come from doing everything. It comes from doing the right things consistently, with a plan you understand.

That's how I coach now. I help you build a physique you're proud of while understanding what you're doing, why you're doing it, and how to keep moving forward.
