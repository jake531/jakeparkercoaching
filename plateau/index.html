What I’d change first

The problem with more effort

Getting lighter isn't always the same as getting leaner.

If your food intake has no clear structure, your workouts aren't building muscle, or your plan keeps falling apart, pushing harder at the same approach may not help.

Eating without clear targets

Eating healthy is good, but you still need to know how much you’re eating and what you’re aiming for.

Working out without progression

Completing workouts doesn't automatically mean you're challenging your muscles in a way you can measure.

Starting over every few weeks

If you keep resetting after a few days, you never really find out if the plan works.

Before changing everything again, let’s look at what’s actually going wrong.
