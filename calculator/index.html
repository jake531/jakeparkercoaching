Why this starting point?

These numbers are a STARTING POINT.

Having a calorie and macro target is better than having no plan at all. But these numbers are still just estimates.

There's already more fitness information online than you could ever need. If you don't know how to actually APPLY that information, another set of numbers isn't going to magically change your physique.

What matters is what happens AFTER you get these numbers. Can you consistently follow them? How is your body responding? What's happening with your weight, digestion, energy, hunger, performance, etc.?

Because these numbers WILL change. Without consistently gathering feedback, you're guessing every time you make an adjustment.

The goal isn't to find some perfect set of macros. It's to establish a starting point, EXECUTE, gather feedback, and make adjustments based on what your body is actually doing.

The part a calculator misses

Your macros are a starting point. Your body's response is the real feedback.

Two people can follow the same calorie target and respond differently. Even your own needs change as your weight, activity, and training change. A formula can't see what happens after you start eating those numbers.

What you still need to watch

Your weight trend, digestion, energy, hunger, recovery, and gym performance. That's the information that helps you decide what to keep and what to adjust.
